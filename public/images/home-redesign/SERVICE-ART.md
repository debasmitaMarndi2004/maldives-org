# Service card artwork

The following original thematic illustrations were generated with OpenAI Image Gen on 2026-11-10 for the Maldives.org homepage redesign:

- `service-flights.png`: generic unbranded passenger aircraft over tropical turquoise water and clouds.
- `service-stays.png`: generic Maldivian-style overwater bungalow and lagoon.
- `service-activities.png`: generic sea turtle and tropical coral reef.

These are illustrative service-category backgrounds. They do not depict or identify a particular airline, resort, tour operator, named destination, or bookable product. No third-party logos, text, UI, or watermarks were requested. The original generation outputs remain in `/workspace/generated_images`; these files are copies for the application asset pipeline.

Composition: landscape 1672 × 941 px, subjects concentrated on the right with open turquoise space on the left for code-native overlay text. Artwork must not be represented as a verified photograph of a named property or island.

Optimized same-size WebP encodings are provided as `service-flights.webp`, `service-stays.webp`, and `service-activities.webp` (quality 88). They preserve the complete composition; no crop, resizing, or creative image edits were applied. Prefer the WebP versions for the website. Original PNG sources remain in `/workspace/generated_images`.
